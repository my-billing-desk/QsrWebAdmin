# QSR Web Admin & POS

A comprehensive, cloud-based Point of Sale (POS) and administration platform designed for Quick Service Restaurants.

## 🚀 Software Features

- **Order-Type Setup** (Dine-in / Delivery / Take-away)
- **KOT Generation**
- **Billing Operations**
- **Cloud-based Billing software**
- **User-friendly Interface** in a PoS platform
- **Online and Offline modes** (PoS)
- **Keyboard-based and Touch-based interfaces** available
- **User-based roles and privileges**
- **Ebill feature** - Send digital bills
- **Menu Management** with Customization
- **Table Management** (Live) View
- **Desktop Reports** (Sales Summary, Item, Category and more)
- **CRM** for customers insights
- **Area-wise Pricing Module**
- **Live view** of Dine-in/Delivery/Take-away Orders and KOTs
- **SMS alerts** on daily sales volume
- **Multiple-Billing stations** (Screens)
- **Admin control** for all of your outlets in single-click
- **Live view** of (Dine-in / Delivery / Take-away) business
- **Multiple-outlet Sales summary**
- **Real-time statistics** of billing


Admin Menus
1. Dashboard (Main)
Dashboard: Main analytics overview.
Daily Operations (Group):
Running Orders: Live tracking of active orders.
All Orders: Historical order database.
Online Orders: Aggregator orders (Zomato/Swiggy).
KOT: Kitchen Order Ticket management.
Due Payment Settlement: Managing credit/unpaid bills.
Profit & Loss: Daily operational financial view.
2. Menu Management
Menu & Discounts: Item, Category, and Availability management.
Special Note: Pre-defined kitchen instructions.
3. Inventory
Inventory: Full stock, purchase, and recipe management system.
4. Reports
Day End Summary: Closing Z-Report.
Other Reports: Custom operational reports.
Delivery Management: Tracking logistics and riders.
Stock History: Detailed inventory logs.
Profit / Loss: Monthly detailed financial reporting.
5. Promo
Coupons: Promotional codes and discounts.
Gift Cards: Prepaid card management.
Discount Plan: Programmatic discount rules.
Discount: General promotional settings.
6. Financials
Expenses: Daily payout and petty cash tracking.
7. Management (Admin)
Configuration (Sub-Group):
Outlet Configuration, Sub Order Type, Delivery Distance, Area Delivery Charges, Floor Plan (Tables), Email Templates, Subscription Plans.
Accounting (Sub-Group):
Payments, Virtual Wallet, GST Info, Bank/KYC Details, Utility Bills, Expense & Withdrawal, Service History, Agreement/Loan Info, Denomination.
User Management (Sub-Group):
Users (Staff List), System Roles (Permissions).
User Logs (Sub-Group):
Auto Accept Logs, Support, Notifications, Menu Triggers, Closing Hour Logs, Activity Timeline.
Other APPs: External integrations.
Marketplace: Connected restaurant services.
8. CRM
Marketing & Automation: Campaign management and auto-marketing.
Reputation: Customer reviews and feedback.
Customers: Detailed guest database and profiles.
Loyalty: Points-based reward system.
Ebill Templates: Digital invoice customization.
9. Quick Links (Custom)
A user-customizable section where you can pin any of the above options for faster access (e.g., placing "Stock Purchase" or "Day End" at the top)
